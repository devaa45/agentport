Port Stamps

Stamps mark how often you have shown up. They are small tokens that live in your wallet and cannot be transferred or sold — like your Agent ID, they belong to the wallet that earned them.

How it works

1. Open this page in a wallet that holds an Agent ID (for example the Binance Wallet browser) and connect.

2. Check in once a day. Days follow UTC, and consecutive days build your streak.

3. At 1, 3, 7 and 14 total check-ins, claim the matching Port Stamp.

The contract behind this page has no owner and no admin keys. It holds no funds, charges nothing, and never asks for approvals. It only writes down who showed up, and when.
